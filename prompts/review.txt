The attached images are frames sampled from the middle of each scene, in order. Captions in a single frame show only part of the narration; that is expected.

Check:

1. Factual accuracy: every claim and number is supported by the research
2. Script quality: strong hook, clear story, simple language
3. Visual quality in the frames: text is readable, not cut off or overlapping, layouts fit the content
4. Consistency between narration, on-screen text and charts; numbers shown on screen have a source credit
5. Whether the video satisfies the original request
6. Potential copyright or sensitive-content issues

For every problem, add an issue with:
- severity: "minor" (cosmetic or nice-to-have), "major" (a viewer would be misled or confused; must be fixed before publishing) or "critical" (must not be published)
- target_agent: who must fix it: "script" (narration content, accuracy, length), "visual" (storyboard: on-screen text, layout choice, chart data, source credits) or "video" (rendering defects such as cut-off or overlapping text)

Set "approved" to true only if there are no major or critical issues.
"score" is the overall quality from 1 to 10. "feedback" summarises the concrete changes needed.
